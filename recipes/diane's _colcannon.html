Diane's Colcannon

Colcannon is a traditional Irish dish consisting of buttery mashed potatoes with cabbage or kale.

Ingredients:
  • Vegetables: cabbage, potatoes, onion.
  • Bacon, Milk, Seasonings, Butter.
Steps:
  1. Boil and drain the potatoes.
  2. Cook the bacon and reserve drippings.
  3. Sauté cabbage and onion.
  4. Mash potatoes with milk and fold ingredients.
