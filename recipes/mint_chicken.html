Mint Chicken

Fresh lemon juice and mint leaves are a must! Serve over white rice.

Ingredients:
  • Chicken breast, garlic, flour, margarine, lemon juice, mint leaves.
Steps:
  1. Coat chicken in flour and sauté until browned.
  2. Add mint and lemon juice, cover and steam for 5 minutes.
